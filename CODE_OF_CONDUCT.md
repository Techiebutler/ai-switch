# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ai-switch to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- publishing someone else's private information (including their emails or tokens) without permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a [private security report](https://github.com/Techiebutler/ai-switch/security/advisories/new) (it reaches maintainers only; mention it is a conduct report). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
